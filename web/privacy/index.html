Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Bansify Files is a file manager. It works on the files already on your device. This page explains exactly what that means.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no account, no sign-in, no analytics SDK, no crash reporter and no advertising library. We do not receive a list of your files, your usage, your device identifiers or your location.

What the app reads

  • File names, sizes, timestamps and contents of folders you open — read on the device, to draw the screen.
  • Media metadata (for example an image thumbnail) when you preview a file.

None of this leaves your device. There is no server for the app to send it to.

Permissions and why

  • All files access — required to browse folders outside the app's own storage. Without it the app can only see its own directory.
  • Read media — to show images, video and audio in collections and previews.

You can revoke these at any time in Android settings; the app then shows only what it is allowed to see.

Deleting files

Deletions happen on your device, after an explicit confirmation, and are performed by Android's own file APIs. Nothing is uploaded or copied elsewhere first. Deleted files may still be recoverable by your system until the storage is reused — the app does not claim to shred data.

Links out

The app may open your browser for documentation or a release page. Those sites have their own policies; this one covers the app only.

Children

The app is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the date above changes with it and the new version appears at this address. Because the app collects nothing, we do not expect a change that affects you.
