# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a place where anyone can contribute without being harassed,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, caste, gender identity or expression,
level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, religion or sexual identity.

## Expected behaviour

- be direct about code, kind about people
- assume good faith; ask before assuming a mistake was careless
- give and accept technical criticism gracefully
- respect that maintainers have limited time

## Unacceptable behaviour

- harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- discriminatory jokes or comments
- publishing someone's private information
- sustained disruption of discussions
- any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Report problems to **conduct@bansify.com**. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers
may warn, remove comments, or block an account, depending on severity.
